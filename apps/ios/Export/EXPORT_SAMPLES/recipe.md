# Weeknight Miso Salmon

Crispy salmon with a sweet-savory miso glaze. Weeknight fast.

Servings: 4
Time: prep 10 min · cook 20 min

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets (170g each)
- 3 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp salt

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 220C. Line a tray with foil.
2. Whisk miso and mirin; glaze the salmon.
3. Roast 10 min until just opaque. Rest 2 min and serve.
